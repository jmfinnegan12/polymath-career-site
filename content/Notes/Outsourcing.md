# Outsourcing

Not every task needs to be done by you. If something doesn't require your specific knowledge or judgment, it can probably be handed off — and for less than you'd expect.

Platforms like Upwork make it easy to hire for specific deliverables: design, research, bookkeeping, video editing. Scope the task, hand it off, get it back. No overhead, no long-term commitment.

The question isn't "can I do this?" It's "should I be the one doing this?"
